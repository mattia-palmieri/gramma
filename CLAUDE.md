# Gramma

A local-first English speaking coach. It reads every dictation from Handy (open-source speech-to-text) and gives short grammar and fluency feedback, with a local LLM (Ollama). Hackathon MVP, designed to grow into a standalone open-source product.

**Your role:** build and maintain the coach. False corrections are worse than missed errors. Keep it small (YAGNI).

Read `docs/index.md` first. It maps the docs.
